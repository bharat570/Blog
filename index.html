Artificial Intelligence

Most people have a misconception that AI means robots. But that's not the case. AI is something that can think, create, and make data-driven decisions far better than humans could — but only in the digital world. AI can be integrated into robots or machines to make them perform tasks that humans cannot do easily or quickly.

Understanding key terms under AI:

  1. Machine Learning
    • Allows computers to learn from data without being explicitly programmed.
    • Finds patterns in data and improves over time.
    • Example: Email spam filters that get smarter with experience.
  2. Deep Learning
    • Uses neural networks with many layers to learn complex data patterns.
    • Powers voice assistants, image recognition, and autonomous vehicles.
    • Needs huge amounts of data and computational power.
  3. Generative AI
    • Creates new content like text, images, or videos from data.
    • Examples: ChatGPT and Gemini.
  4. Data Science
    • Combines statistics, programming, and domain knowledge to interpret data.
    • Supports AI with reliable and structured data.
